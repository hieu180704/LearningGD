1. ONE-SHEET / PITCH DOC
=========================================================
Nguồn: Press Start Leadership — "From One Sheet to Pitch Deck and Beyond".
URL: https://pressstartleadership.com/from-one-sheet-to-pitch-deck-and-beyond-building-game-pitches-that-land-deals/

2. ONE-PAGE DESIGN — STONE LIBRANDE
=========================================================
One-page design khác one-sheet ở mục 1: tài liệu 1 trang cho ĐỘI DEV dùng nội bộ, không
phải tài liệu pitch cho nhà đầu tư/publisher.

2.1. GDC Vault — session "One-Page Designs", GDC 2010. Speaker: Stone Librande, company
ghi trên trang: EA/Maxis.
URL: https://gdcvault.com/play/1012356/One-Page

[NV] (đây là MÔ TẢ SESSION do GDC viết, dạng câu hỏi tu từ — KHÔNG phải lời Librande nói
trực tiếp, luôn phải ghi rõ điều này khi trích dẫn):
"In an effort to communicate more effectively and concisely, Stone has been experimenting
with a style of design documentation that he calls a one page design. As the name implies,
this is a document that is exactly one page long. After all, why create a document with
more than one page if most people only read the first page anyway?"

[NV] "During this talk Stone will show numerous examples of one page designs from
Diablo III, The Simpsons and Spore."

CẢNH BÁO — MINH HOẠ THEO NĂM: talk 2010 minh hoạ bằng Diablo III / The Simpsons / Spore.
SimCity thuộc một talk KHÁC, năm 2013 ("Simulating a City - One Page at a Time") — không
được gộp chung hai talk này khi viết bài.

2.2. Game Developer — bài tổng thuật talk trên, tác giả Danielle Riendeau.
URL: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/-the-goal-of-design-is-to-efficiently-communicate-ideas-

[NV] "Responding to the age-old design problems of thick "design bibles" and the

CẢNH BÁO — CẤM GÁN CÂU TIÊU ĐỀ: câu "The goal of design is to efficiently communicate

3. GDD — TIM RYAN VÀ PHÊ PHÁN GDD DÀY
=========================================================
3.1. Tim Ryan — "The Anatomy of a Design Document, Part 1: Documentation Guidelines for
the Game Concept and Proposal", Gamasutra, 19/10/1999. Nguồn sơ cấp, chắc nhất về GDD.
URL: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-anatomy-of-a-design-document-part-1-documentation-guidelines-for-the-game-concept-and-proposal

[NV] "In broad terms, the purpose of documentation is to communicate the vision in
sufficient detail to implement it."

[NV] "A design document is a bible from which the producer preaches the goal, through
which the designers champion their ideas, and from which the artists and programmers get
their instructions..."

[NV] "Design documents merely express the consensus, flesh out the ideas, and eliminate
the vagueness. They themselves are discussion pieces. Though they strive to cement ideas
and plans, they are not carved in stone."

[NV] Mục của Game Concept: "Introduction / Background (optional) / Description / Key
features / Genre / Platform(s) / Concept art (optional)"

[NV] Mục của Game Proposal: "Revised game concept / Market analysis / Technical analysis /
Legal analysis (if applicable) / Cost and revenue projections / Art"

3.2. Codecks blog — "Writing Modern Game Design Documents". Blog của công ty làm tool
quản lý dự án game; không xác định được tên tác giả cụ thể.
URL: https://www.codecks.io/blog/writing-modern-game-design-documents/

[NV] "In doubt your game is your GDD."
[NV] "Large documents with dozens of pages can be hard to read."
[NV] "get out of sync immediately"

3.3. Mark Cerny — "The Method", trình bày tại D.I.C.E Summit 2002.
NGUỒN LÀ BÀI TÓM TẮT BÊN THỨ BA, không phải bản ghi gốc: Iterative (tác giả Joe Mahoney),

CẢNH BÁO — NGUỒN THỨ CẤP + SAI SỰ KIỆN NẾU GHI NHẦM: câu "production is easy, but
pre-production is hard" đến từ bài tóm tắt bên thứ ba viết năm 2022, KHÔNG phải bản ghi
gốc lời Mark Cerny. Talk diễn ra tại D.I.C.E Summit 2002, KHÔNG phải GDC. Khi dùng, phải

3.4. Rob Sandberg — "Why Game Design Docs Still Matter, Even for LiveOps", Production
Alchemist, 23/4/2025.
URL: https://www.productionalchemist.com/p/why-game-design-docs-still-matter

[NV] "...when stakeholders like publishers, licensors, or investors need updates, your GDD

trust."

[NV] "LiveOps, on the other hand, runs on speed and iteration. The documentation needs to
be lean and modular, focused on the update, the goal, and the technical setup."

4. SPEC — FUNCTIONAL VS TECHNICAL (TIM RYAN)
=========================================================
Tim Ryan — "The Anatomy of a Design Document, Part 2: Documentation Guidelines for the
Functional and Technical Specifications", Gamasutra, 17/12/1999.
URL: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-anatomy-of-a-design-document-part-2-documentation-guidelines-for-the-functional-and-technical-specifications
(Ghi chú kỹ thuật: URL này do main agent tự tìm ra; một agent research trước đó từng kết
luận nhầm là bài đã không còn tồn tại — không lặp lại kết luận đó.)

[NV] "In short, what goes into the game and what it does is documented in the functional
specification. This is often written from the perspective of the user. How it is
implemented and how it performs the function is documented in the technical specification.
This is often written from the system perspective."

[NV] "...the functional specification should have joint ownership. This keeps people from
feeling isolated and more a part of the process, and it makes the documents feel less like
marching orders and more like a plan. The team members are also much more likely to read
something that they helped put together."

[NV] "Traditionally in the game industry, there was only one spec. How technical it was
depended on who wrote it."
